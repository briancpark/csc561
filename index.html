Computer Graphics Projects

Five projects from Principles of Computer Graphics — from a software ray caster written from scratch to a shadow-mapped WebGL2 game. Everything renders live in your browser; each page lists its controls. The blobs behind this text are ray-marched in a fragment shader, and your cursor is the light — move it around, or click to scatter them.
